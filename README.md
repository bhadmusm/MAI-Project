# MAI Project Repositry

Master Project by Maryam Bhadmus.

## Description
This project explores how to better predict and analyse the energy used by data centers 
to help data center operators in planning energy usage of different components such as 
cooling and hardware, while also reducing costs and the environmental impact. Three 
technology models are used: including a simple model(Linear regression) and more 
advanced models(CNN-LSTM and Transformer) and the impact of different components 
such as CPU, GPU and RAM were studied. The results of the project show that the 
advanced Transformer model had the best performance by having the most accurate 
predictions to better predict spikes in energy usage. The insights gained from the 
investigation can help in guiding data centers to make better decisions for saving 
electricity and shrinking their carbon footprint worldwide.
